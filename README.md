# AI Governance Risk Assessment — COSO ERM

## Overview
An individual academic case study examining AI governance risks through Deloitte Australia’s reported AI-assisted government report errors.

I applied the COSO Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) framework to analyze risks, assess potential control gaps, and propose stronger verification and oversight.

## Business Question
How can professional services organizations manage the risk of inaccurate AI-generated content in high-stakes client deliverables?

## My Contribution
I completed the research, risk assessment, risk register, control recommendations, and presentation independently.

## Analytical Approach
- Reviewed published reporting on the incident.
- Organized the analysis around COSO ERM components.
- Assessed potential operational, strategic, reputational, and governance risks.
- Developed a qualitative risk register.
- Proposed controls, monitoring indicators, and escalation mechanisms.

## Risks Examined
| Risk | Potential consequence |
|---|---|
| Fabricated citations and inaccurate claims | Unreliable deliverables and reputational harm |
| Unclear responsibility for verification | Errors passing through review |
| Insufficient AI-use transparency | Loss of client trust |
| Delivery pressure overriding review | Inconsistent quality control |
| Policies without effective implementation | Gaps between governance expectations and practice |

## Proposed Controls
- Require human verification of AI-generated citations and material claims.
- Maintain a documented review and sign-off trail.
- Conduct independent quality assurance reviews.
- Establish clear AI oversight responsibilities.
- Define disclosure requirements and approved AI use cases.
- Escalate material errors before publication.

## Proposed Monitoring Indicators
- Percentage of AI-assisted deliverables receiving documented review
- Percentage of audited reports containing factual or citation errors
- Time required to resolve identified errors
- Percentage of high-stakes AI projects receiving formal oversight review

These indicators are proposed measures, not observed organizational results.

## Limitations
This is an academic analysis based on public reporting, not an internal audit of Deloitte.

Statements about internal governance and control failures represent analytical interpretations unless supported by cited evidence. Recommendations and numerical thresholds are illustrative proposals; their effectiveness has not been tested.

The presentation uses COSO ERM components. Some principle labels and mappings require refinement: Principle 13 is “Implements Risk Responses,” and ongoing quality assurance aligns more directly with Principle 16, “Reviews Risk and Performance.”
